Right now...

📍 Based in San Diego, California

I'm currently studying Cognitive Science at UC San Diego. These days, you'll usually find me coding this website, working on personal projects, wandering around with my camera, or trying to figure out what kind of creator I want to become.

It started here.

One of my favorite childhood photos.

I grew up around computers long before I knew what UX design was. Some of my happiest memories were sitting beside my mom, drawing in MS Paint, learning to type, and wondering what every button did.

Looking back, I think curiosity found me long before I found a career.

Growing up...

Growing up, life wasn't really about discovering who I was.

It was about getting through each stage of life and doing what needed to be done.

My twenties have been different.

For the first time, I've had the chance to ask myself what genuinely excites me, what I enjoy creating, and what kind of life I want to build.

Why this place exists.

This website started as a portfolio.

Somewhere along the way, it became something more.

It's now a place where I can collect the things I've built, the ideas I've explored, the little moments that inspire me, and hopefully many more stories in the years to come.

Looking ahead...

I don't know exactly where life will take me.

What I do know is that I enjoy creating.

Whether it's designing, drawing, photography, filmmaking, or something I haven't discovered yet, I hope whatever I make gives someone a reason to smile or look forward to tomorrow.
